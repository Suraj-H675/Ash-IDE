**Date:** 2026-11-16 (Asia/Kolkata). **Base:** clean, synchronized canonical `main` at `61593b0ec52d1d00fe0b8b923de8568cc24b7605`; [CI 43747130101](https://github.com/Suraj-H675/Ash-IDE/actions/runs/43747130101) passed for that exact baseline. **Branch:** `fix/m8-prune-retired-mobile-rpc-manifest`, isolated worktree. **Pinned source:** read-only Orca `55697aec91390357540103f49991458f756b41cc`, original MIT notice and provenance ledger retained.

## Scope and safety decision

The inherited `ALL_RPC_METHODS` eagerly imported five groups even though `ASH_RPC_METHODS` already filtered their names: `EMULATOR_METHODS`, `PAIRING_METHODS`, `MOBILE_RELAY_HOSTS_METHODS`, `MOBILE_PROJECT_SECTIONS_METHODS`, and `MOBILE_WEB_BUNDLE_METHODS`. Removed their imports and registrations from the broad server manifest. These represent excluded mobile app/emulator, phone pairing, remote relay, mobile sidebar and mobile-web delivery workflows, **not** local PTY, manual orchestration, browser extensions, Git-over-SSH, the local runtime or normal Chromium browser.

A new focused regression first **failed on the old code** and then passed after the registry change. It checks both that the broad manifest does not load those five method groups and that neither registry registers their method names, while preserving `browser.tabCreate` and `orchestration.run`. The type-level uncatalogued-method check no longer requires `emulator.install`, an intentionally retired RPC, but still requires retained orchestration methods. Source files and legacy-generated RPC parameter metadata are not deleted here; the latter needs separate dependency-reviewed pruning.

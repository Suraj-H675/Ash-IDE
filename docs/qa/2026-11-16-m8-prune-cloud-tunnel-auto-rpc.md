## Decision and source boundary

`src/main/runtime/rpc/methods/index.ts` previously eager-imported and spread `CONNECTOR_METHODS` (`connector.*` cloud/external conversations), `BROWSER_NETWORK_TUNNEL_METHODS` (`network.browserTunnel` paired-client browser network stream), and `AUTOMATIC_WORK_METHODS` (`runtime.automaticWork.*` inherited automatic work status/resume) into `ALL_RPC_METHODS`, even though the authenticated `ASH_RPC_METHODS` filter already excluded every one of their names. Removed precisely these three imports and spreads.

A regression in `ash-runtime-methods.test.ts` enumerates all methods in the three defining groups, proves none is present in either manifest, rejects any corresponding central-registry source reference, and protects browser-tab, manual orchestration, Git, PTY, desktop-browser-bridge and updater registrations. The regression **failed once on the unchanged base** and passed after the change. No underlying implementation source, shared type schemas, bridge wiring, updater, provider-native skills/MCP, normal Git-over-SSH, or CLI was removed. `MANAGED_RUNTIME_INSTALL_METHODS` is intentionally untouched pending review of real agent-runtime setup dependencies. `HOSTED_REVIEW_METHODS` is retained because Git-provider workflows are approved.
